Welcome to Miseva AI — your intelligent culinary companion, inspired by the French culinary discipline of mise en place (everything prepared before cooking) and devoted Indian service (seva).

Whether you are a beginner stepping into the kitchen for the first time, a seasoned cook perfecting an authentic Kerala Sadya, or a dinner host looking to create an unforgettable table setting, Miseva AI transforms cooking into a seamless, joyful journey of cultural discovery and culinary precision.

🌟 KEY FEATURES

1. 270+ VERIFIED WORLD & REGIONAL INDIAN RECIPES
Explore verified recipes with guaranteed minimum 10 distinct authentic dishes across every published collection:
• Regional Indian: Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh & Telangana, Punjab, Maharashtra, Gujarat, West Bengal, and Himalayan heritage.
• World Cuisines: Authentic Italian, Japanese, Mexican, Chinese, Thai, French, and Middle Eastern gastronomic traditions.

2. BILINGUAL ENGLISH & MALAYALAM AI VOICE GUIDANCE
Cook completely hands-free! Our intelligent voice engine provides:
• Synchronized voice narration in natural English and native Malayalam (മലയാളം ലിപി).
• Hands-free voice commands: Say "Next step", "Repeat step", or "Set timer" without touching your phone with flour-dusted hands.
• Active step highlighting with karaoke-style text tracking.

3. SCREEN-AWAKE COOKING STUDIO
Never struggle with screen lockouts while cooking. The active Cooking Studio mode automatically keeps your phone screen illuminated while you prepare dishes, with active step timers for baking, braising, and tempering.

4. 100% OFFLINE ROOM DATABASE
Weak Wi-Fi in the kitchen? Miseva AI’s local SQLite Room database caches your saved recipes, meal plans, and instructions so you can cook completely offline with zero interruptions.

5. DEDICATED DESSERTS & CONFECTIONERY STUDIO
Master 40 artisanal sweets across 4 subcategories:
• Traditional Payasam & Kheer (Palada, Ada Pradhaman, Parippu).
• Regional Indian Mithai (Gulab Jamun, Mysore Pak, Rasgulla).
• Cakes & Bakes with exact oven temperatures and pan conversions.
• Global Delights with precise sugar-syrup thread tests.

6. ARTISANAL BEVERAGES & JUICES STUDIO
Ditch artificial sodas with 50 handcrafted drinks:
• Cold-pressed fresh fruit juices and detox smoothies.
• Traditional heritage coolers (Sambharam, Solkadhi, Shikanji).
• Artisanal mocktails, brewed teas, and filter coffees with hot/cold filters.

7. TABLE STYLING & TABLESCAPING STUDIO
Move beyond the skillet! Design stunning dining experiences with 40 complete concepts:
• Authentic Kerala Sadya with banana leaf layouts and brass Nilavilakku placement.
• Royal Indian banquets, intimate candlelit dinners, and modern minimalist outdoor tables.
• Interactive 2D top-down arrangement canvas with automated tableware checklists.

8. LIVE INDIAN GROCERY PRICE COMPARISON
Compare live ingredient prices across 6 leading Indian retailers:
• Swiggy Instamart, Blinkit, Zepto, Amazon Fresh, Flipkart Grocery, and BigBasket.
• View true recipe consumption cost vs. checkout pack size estimates.
