## 2. Anatomy & Symbolism

```
             ▲
           /   \        Flame (Saffron Gold #D97706)
          |  *  |  <--- Hearth, Heat & Passionate Cooking
  Leaf   /   ●   \      Inner AI Node (#FFFFFF / #F59E0B)
 (Emerald\       /      Intelligence, Algorithms & Voice
 #059669) \     /

```

1. **The Flame:** Represents culinary heat, tempering (*Tadka*), reduction, and baking precision.
2. **The Herb Leaf:** Represents fresh farm-to-table produce, raw ingredients, and *mise en place*.
3. **The AI Node:** Represents intelligent culinary reasoning, conversational voice guidance, and price calculation.
4. **The Outer Glass Ring:** Reflects modern liquid-glass aesthetics and premium hospitality.

## 3. Clear Space & Minimum Sizing
- **Clear Space:** Maintain clear margin of at least `0.5X` around the logo mark, where `X` is the diameter of the circular emblem.
- **Minimum Digital Size:**
  - Horizontal Wordmark: Minimum `140px` wide on digital displays.
  - Symbol Mark: Minimum `24px × 24px` on mobile UI icons; `16px × 16px` for browser favicons.
  - Print Minimum: `25mm` width for horizontal logo; `10mm` for icon mark.

---

## 4. Logo Don'ts
- ❌ **Do not** stretch, skew, or distort the aspect ratio of the emblem or wordmark.
- ❌ **Do not** replace the custom "Plus Jakarta Sans" wordmark with generic serif or script fonts.
- ❌ **Do not** invert the flame and leaf positions.
- ❌ **Do not** apply harsh drop shadows or neon glows that clash with the liquid-glass aesthetic.
- ❌ **Do not** place the dark-mode logo on white backgrounds without adequate contrast.
